# HEADLINE RULES

Headlines should feel:
- conversational
- newsroom-human
- slightly tired
- observational
- compact

NOT:
- SEO spam
- clickbait
- corporate
- sensationalist
- AI-generated sounding

---

# GOOD ENERGY

Feels like:
- a friend sending a link
- someone reacting in WhatsApp
- “mae vea esto”

---

# AVOID

BAD:
- “Impactante”
- “No podrás creer”
- “Revelación”
- “Usuarios explotan”
- “Internet reacciona”

Avoid:
- ragebait
- engagement bait
- exaggerated drama

---

# PREFERRED STYLE

Prefer:
- understated irony
- conversational phrasing
- emotionally aware wording
- small absurdity

Examples:

“Diay sí… ahora también cerrarán esto de noche.”

“Legalmente ya nadie entiende este trámite.”

“Otro día normal en Circunvalación.”

“Uno intentando ahorrar y sale esto.”

---

# LENGTH

Prefer:
- short
- scannable
- screenshotable

Usually:
- 4–12 words
