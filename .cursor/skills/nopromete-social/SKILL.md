# SOCIAL MEDIA RULES

Social posts should:
- feel native
- feel human
- feel shareable
- feel screenshotable

NOT:
- corporate
- over-produced
- LinkedIn-like
- AI motivational

---

# ENERGY

The vibe:
- group chat
- “too real”
- Costa Rican millennial exhaustion
- emotionally aware humor

---

# DON ZOPI

Don Zopi should:
- react
- observe
- emotionally process

He does NOT:
- lecture
- explain politics
- preach

---

# CAPTION STYLE

Short.
Punchy.
Conversational.

Avoid:
- giant paragraphs
- hashtags overload
- cringe CTA

---

# GOOD EXAMPLES

“Costa Rica convirtiendo cualquier vuelta en side quest.”

“Legalmente uno ocupa hidratación emocional para manejar aquí.”

“El país entero sobreviviendo a punta de café.”

---

# CTA STYLE

Soft CTAs only.

GOOD:
- “Diay sí…”
- “Qué cansado legalmente.”
- “Mae ustedes cómo hacen.”

BAD:
- “SMASH LIKE”
- “FOLLOW FOR MORE”
- “COMMENT BELOW”
